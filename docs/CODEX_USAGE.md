## Guardrails

Codex-generated explanations do not determine academic correctness. GPT output is constrained to structured extraction, read-only inspection tools, and advisor-facing explanation; the deterministic engine and evidence states govern displayed routes. Seeded content is labeled and remains available when live execution is not configured.
